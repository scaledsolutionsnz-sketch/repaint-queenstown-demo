Privacy Policy

How Repaint Queenstown handles the personal information you share when you get in touch about a painting job.

Placeholder content for this demo. Repaint Queenstown should review and replace this wording so it matches how the business actually handles customer information before the site goes live.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers personal information collected by Repaint Queenstown ("we", "us") through this website and through enquiries about painting and decorating work in Queenstown and the Wakatipu Basin. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 2020.

2. What we collect

We only collect what we need to quote and carry out work:

  • Your name, phone number and email address when you call or email us.
  • The property address and details of the work you want done.
  • Photos you send us of the areas to be painted, and photos we take on site for quoting and record purposes.
  • Basic, non-identifying technical information your browser sends when you visit this site.

This website does not have any contact or feedback forms. Enquiries reach us by phone or email only.

3. How we use it

We use your information to prepare quotes, schedule and carry out work, order materials, invoice you, and follow up about the job. We may contact you afterwards about workmanship or a warranty matter. We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to marketing lists without your agreement.

4. Who we share it with

We may share limited information with suppliers or subcontractors where it is needed to complete your job, for example, ordering a matched colour or arranging a specialist repair. We may also share information with our accountant or where the law requires it.

5. Job photos

We sometimes photograph completed work for our website or social media. Photos are only used where the property is not identified by address, and we will not publish photos of the inside of your home without asking you first. Tell us at any time if you would rather we didn't, and we will remove them.

6. Storing your information

Information is kept on our phone, email and accounting systems, which are password protected. We keep records for as long as we need them for the job, for warranty questions and for tax and record-keeping requirements.

7. Cookies and analytics

This site keeps tracking to a minimum. Where third-party services are embedded, such as Google Maps or web fonts, those providers may set their own cookies and receive standard request information. Their own privacy policies apply to that use.

8. Access and correction

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Contact Sam on 022 694 7311 or email us through the contact section and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version published on this page applies from the date it is posted.
