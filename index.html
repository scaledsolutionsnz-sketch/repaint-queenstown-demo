What we do

Painting work, named the way you'd ask for it

Most of our work is repaints — houses that have taken ten years of sun, frost and southerly and need bringing back rather than built from scratch.

01

Exterior repaints

Weatherboard, cedar, plaster and joinery. Washed back, sanded, primed where it's needed, then finish coats rated for alpine sun and frost.

02

Interior repaints

Walls, ceilings, doors and skirtings. Furniture covered and shifted back, and rooms handed over clean — not left for you to sort.

03

Prep, patching & repairs

Filling, sanding, rot and rust spots sorted, gaps sealed and joinery made good. The part that decides whether the paint lasts three years or ten.

04

Roof & spouting recoats

Long-run and tile roofs washed down and recoated, spouting and fascia brought back to match, so the roof stops ageing the whole house.

05

Colour matching & advice

Bring the swatch or we'll match what's already there. Sample pots go on the wall and get looked at in morning and evening light before you commit.

06

Rentals & holiday homes

Turnarounds scheduled around bookings and tenants, with the place cleaned up and ready to let the day we finish.

About us

Painters who'd rather spend the extra day on prep

Repaint Queenstown is a painting and decorating outfit working across Queenstown and the Wakatipu Basin. Houses here cop it — hard UV off the lake in summer, frost and snow load through winter, and weatherboards that dry out and open up. A tidy top coat over poor prep looks great for a season and then starts letting go.

So we quote the preparation as clearly as the paint: what gets washed, what gets sanded back, what gets filled or replaced, and what goes on top. You'll know what you're paying for before anyone lifts a brush.

  • Queenstown based, working the whole Wakatipu Basin
  • Written quote with the prep spelled out line by line
  • Same person from the quote through to the final coat
