How we work

From first phone call to the final walk-through

Repaints go sideways when nobody agrees what "prep" means. Here's exactly how a job runs with us, so there are no surprises in week two.

The process

Four steps, no guesswork

01

Look at the job

We come and walk the house with you: what's flaking, what's rotten, what only needs a wash and a recoat. Ten minutes of honesty here saves a lot of money later.

02

Written quote

You get the scope in writing: the prep, the products, the number of coats and the price. If something changes on site you hear about it before it's done, not on the invoice.

03

Prep and paint

Wash, sand, fill, seal and mask, then coats go on in order. Drop sheets down each morning, gear packed away each evening, and the house stays liveable throughout.

04

Walk it together

We go around the job with you at the end, mark anything you want touched up, and sort it before the gear leaves the driveway.

Exterior work

What's included

What a repaint actually covers

Every quote is written for the house in front of us, but this is the shape of a standard exterior or interior repaint in the Wakatipu.

  • Wash down, scrape back and sand any failing coating
  • Fill, seal and make good, including rot and rust spots we find
  • Primer or sealer where bare substrate is exposed
  • Two finish coats in the colour and sheen you've signed off
  • Masking, drop sheets and a tidy site every evening
  • Final walk-through and touch-ups before we pack up

Good to know

The questions we get asked most

When's the best time to paint outside?

Spring through autumn is the sweet spot in the basin, you need dry surfaces and overnight temperatures that let the coating cure. Interior work carries on right through winter.

Do we need to move out?

Almost never. We work room by room, cover and shift furniture, and hand each space back usable. Most people stay in the house the whole way through.

How long does a house take?

It depends on how much prep the place needs, not the square metres. We give you a realistic window in the quote and tell you early if weather pushes it.

Can you match the existing colour?

Yes. We can match off an existing painted surface or work from your swatch, and we'll put sample pots up so you can see it in real light before committing.

Do you handle rentals and holiday homes?

Regularly. We schedule around bookings and tenants, and can work to a hard finish date so the place is ready for the next guests.

What about repairs we find mid-job?

You get a call and a price before anything extra happens. Nothing gets added to the invoice that you haven't agreed to first.
